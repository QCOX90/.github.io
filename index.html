Meet the Owner

Susan Smiley, MPT, is the owner of Smiley Physical Therapy and a licensed physical therapist serving the Olive Branch, Mississippi community. She has been an active healthcare provider since 2009, bringing years of experience to her patients.

Susan is dedicated to helping individuals improve mobility, reduce pain, and return to their everyday lives with confidence. Her approach to care focuses on personalized treatment, clear communication, and creating a welcoming environment for every patient.

At Smiley Physical Therapy, patients can expect professional, attentive care centered around their recovery and long-term wellness.
